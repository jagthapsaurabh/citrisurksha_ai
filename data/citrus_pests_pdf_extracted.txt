1. Asian Citrus Psyllid 
Names 
English Asian Citrus Psyllid 
Hindi एशियन साइट्रस शसल्ला 
Marathi श िंबू शसल्ला 
Scientific Name Diaphorina citri 
Overview 
English: 
Asian Citrus Psyllid is one of the economically important citrus pests in India. 
 
श िंदी: 
एशियन साइट्रस शसल्ला भारत के प्रमुख साइट्रस कीट् िं में से एक  ै। 
 
मराठी: 
श िंबू शसल्ला  ा भारताती  श िंबूवर्गीय शिकािंवरी  म त्त्वाचा शकड आ े. 
Identification 
Tiny brown insect (3–4 mm), wings held at 45°, yellow-orange nymphs on tender flush. 
Host Plants 
Orange, Lemon, Lime, Mandarin, Sweet Lime and Grapefruit. 
Damage Symptoms 
Sap-sucking insect and vector of Citrus Greening (HLB). Causes curling of tender leaves, 
stunted flush and reduced yield. 
Life Cycle 
Egg → Immature stage (nymph/larva) → Adult. Multiple generations may occur under warm 
conditions. 
Monitoring 
Inspect young flush and the underside of leaves every week. Record infestation level and 
capture photographs for AI datasets. 
Integrated Pest Management 
Regular monitoring, remove infected shoots, conserve Tamarixia radiata, neem oil, use 
registered systemic insecticides only when ETL is crossed. 
AI Detection Features 
- Images of eggs, immature stages and adults 
- Healthy vs damaged leaves 
- Different lighting, backgrounds and mobile cameras 
- Bounding boxes and segmentation masks
